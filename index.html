Questions practitioners actually ask

My clients won't use another app.

They don't have to. You log the session in ten seconds; they answer four taps at the end of the visit. If a client never opens it, you still get the report and the trend line.

I already keep notes.

Then you're ahead — bring them to the call. The difference is what happens when those notes become a timeline the client can see, hold, and forward. Notes inform you. A timeline keeps them.

Is this a medical record? HIPAA?

It's a wellness journal, not a clinical record, and it makes no medical claims. The app shows the client's own reported scores — never an asserted outcome. We'll sign a simple data processing agreement, and your data stays yours.

Does it work with Pulse equipment?

Yes — it's built around the Pulse product line: protocols, timer, intensity, and session history map to the machines you already run.
